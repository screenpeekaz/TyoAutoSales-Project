Used Car Buying Guide

Buying a used car is easier when you know what information to check before making a decision.

Before You Buy

Start by deciding on a realistic budget and comparing several vehicles instead of focusing on the first option you see. Look at mileage, condition, price, and how the vehicle fits your everyday needs.

  1. Compare the price and mileage with similar vehicles.
  2. Ask about maintenance and service history when available.
  3. Inspect the interior, exterior, tires, and basic vehicle functions.
  4. Research the VIN and check for open safety recalls.

Research the Vehicle

A VIN can help identify important information about a specific vehicle. The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration provides a free VIN decoder that buyers can use as an outside research resource.

Visit the NHTSA VIN Decoder

Take Your Time

A used vehicle is a major purchase, so it is worth comparing your choices and asking questions. If a vehicle on our inventory page interests you, use the Contact page to ask about availability or additional details.
